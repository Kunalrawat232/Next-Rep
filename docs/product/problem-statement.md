# Problem statement

## Problem

People who lift weights need to record what they lifted (exercise, weight, reps) so they can progress over time. Today they use notes apps, paper diaries or tap-heavy apps. Logging between sets is awkward: hands are sweaty or chalky, the phone is locked, and typing breaks focus.

Gyms are often in basements with poor signal, so online-only apps fail at the worst moment.

Weight is also ambiguous: is "45 kg" the total on the bar or the weight per side, and what does the bar weigh at this gym?

## Target user

Regular gym-goers who lift with barbells, dumbbells and machines and want a fast, reliable log of their training and a clear view of their progress.

## Core value

**Say "bench press 60 kilo 8 reps" between sets and it is logged correctly, even with bad signal, with the bar weight and per-side maths handled for you.**

## Product

Working name **Next Rep**: a voice-first gym workout logger, delivered as an installable web app (PWA).
