**Before the code freeze (25 Oct), on 23 to 24 Oct, switch these on** in the Supabase dashboard: "Confirm email" (so duplicate sign-ups stop revealing which addresses are registered, see ADR-007), CAPTCHA at sign-up (account farming, see ADR-017), and leaked-password protection. Also on 23 to 24 Oct: check that confirmation emails really arrive (the free default mail service is limited to about 2 an hour and is best effort), test sign-up and the "check your email" path on a Vercel preview, and keep two pre-confirmed demo accounts for judges, so nobody is blocked by a missing email.

**Decided 2026-10-19:** the graphify knowledge-graph tool is left out until after the 30 Oct submission (about 200 source files, `AGENT_CONTEXT.md` and `agent_docs/` already serve as the map, its install rewrote tracked files, and the freeze is close). It was removed in Phase 0 because the project was too small to benefit (see the AI log, 2026-10-08).
