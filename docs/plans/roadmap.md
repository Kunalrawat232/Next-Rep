**Supabase dashboard settings for Phase 5.** Done: "Confirm email" is on (so duplicate sign-ups stop revealing which addresses are registered, see ADR-007), with custom SMTP through Brevo's free plan (Supabase's built-in mail sends only to members of the Supabase organisation, about 2 an hour), an email limit of 100 an hour, two pre-confirmed demo accounts for judges, and a confirmation email received and signed in with on the Vercel preview. The Site URL is set to the production address https://nextrep-gym.vercel.app.

**Decided:** the graphify knowledge-graph tool is left out until after the 30 Oct submission (about 200 source files, `AGENT_CONTEXT.md` and `agent_docs/` already serve as the map, its install rewrote tracked files, and the freeze is close). It was removed in Phase 0 because the project was too small to benefit (see the AI log).
